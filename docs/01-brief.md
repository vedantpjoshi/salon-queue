# Glamour Queue — Salon Queue Management — Brief

## Problem
Popular salons see 50+ walk-in customers on weekends. Without a queue system,
customers wait without knowing their turn, stylists lose track of bookings,
and the front desk juggles names on paper. Result: long waits, lost customers,
and stressed staff.

## Users
- **Customers** — browse services, book, track token
- **Stylists / Therapists** — see their queue, update status
- **Front-desk / Counter staff** — check out completed customers
- **Salon owner / Manager** — manage services, view daily stats

## Must-have features
1. Browse services by category (Hair, Skin, Nails, Spa) with price & duration
2. Add to cart → enter name & phone → get a token number
3. Live token tracking page for the customer
4. Stylist board to move bookings: New → In Progress → Completed
5. TV "Now Serving" display for the waiting area
6. Owner can add/edit/delete services and mark them "Unavailable"

## Don't build
- Online payment / UPI integration
- Login / signup / Google auth
- Appointment scheduling (future date/time)
- Reviews & ratings
- Loyalty points / coupons
- SMS / email notifications

## Look & feel
Mobile-first, clean and modern, violet/purple accent (#7C3AED),
rounded cards, big readable token numbers.
